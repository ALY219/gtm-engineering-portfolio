## 💼 Case Study Preview: GrowthFlow AI

**Problem:** Solo founder scaling B2B outreach to US/CA/AU real estate teams without a sales team.

**Solution:** Built an AI-native GTM motion from scratch:

- **Infrastructure:** Secondary domains + SPF/DKIM/DMARC hardening → 10/10 Mail-Tester
- **Enrichment:** Scraped + deduped + LLM-scored leads (0–100 prospect score on 10+ signals)
- **Outbound:** 5-day multi-channel cadence (email + LinkedIn + US VoIP SMS)
- **Productization:** 4 fixed-scope service tiers ($75–$250) with 48-hour SLAs
- **Intake Pipeline:** Tally form → Payoneer invoicing → async delivery

**Results:**
- 20–40% email reply rates (10–20× industry benchmark)
- ~14% LinkedIn DM reply rate (2× benchmark)
- 4 paying clients in first 30 days
- Zero paid ads, zero outbound hires

## 🧠 Philosophy: RevOps as Engineering

I treat revenue operations the way senior engineers treat production systems:

- **Instrumentation first:** If you can't measure it, you can't optimize it. Every funnel stage has a metric.
- **Defensive by default:** Deliverability isn't a checkbox — it's infrastructure (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, blacklist monitoring, sender rotation).
- **AI as infrastructure, not gimmick:** LLMs don't replace strategy; they execute strategy at scale with evaluation harnesses.
- **Human-in-the-loop always:** Automation handles volume; humans handle judgment. Every workflow has an approval gate where it matters.
- **Ship artifacts, not slides:** Every day ends with a committed file, a live dashboard, or a shipped workflow.
